Battleship-ML — Live WebAssembly Demo

C++ Battleship AI that blends four probabilistic signals — a log-learned global heatmap, a live ship-placement coverage map, Monte Carlo sampling, and tactical bonuses — to minimize shots-to-win. Monte Carlo runs on a custom CUDA kernel (shared-memory atomics + cuRAND) and falls back to CPU automatically. Multi-threaded CLI tuner supports grid-search and online learning. Also compiles to WebAssembly — watch the AI's probability heatmap update live in the browser.

C++17 CUDA WebAssembly Emscripten Monte Carlo

AutoDetect-Wildlife

CS-280 Robotics Final · 2026

Real-time wildlife detection for forest roads. Runs YOLOv11s at 60–80 FPS on a laptop; deploys to a Raspberry Pi 5 as a custom INT8-quantized ONNX model — no PyTorch required on device. Custom training pipeline merges Roboflow and LILA.science datasets (deer, elk, turkey, moose). Includes a close-animal collision warning and headless MJPEG stream mode.
